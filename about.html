Profile

I'm Sean, a BSIS (BS Information Systems) student at Mandaluyong College of Science and Technology in the Philippines, currently in BSIS-32. My coursework spans data mining, data warehousing, business process management, IT infrastructure and networking, and applied development — but the thread running through all of it is governance, risk, and compliance.

I was recently elected auditor for the entire BSIS program, which put me on the other side of the process-review table earlier than most. It's also the reason this site treats every project like a filed record: a reference code, a status, and evidence to back it up.

My working goal is a cybersecurity or GRC role within two to four years of graduating in 2028. Everything on this site — the portfolio, the skills list, even the format of this page — is built as practice for that.
